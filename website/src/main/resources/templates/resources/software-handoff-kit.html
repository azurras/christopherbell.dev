What is in the full kit?

Six reusable worksheets, a complete fictional worked example, and a printable companion.

Delivery inventory

List the assets, versions, recipient-accessible links, owners, and evidence of receipt.

Access transfer

Record who should control each service and which transfers still need verification.

Acceptance and evidence

Keep agreed checks, observed results, and the receiving owner's decision together.

Operating notes

Document how to find, run, maintain, and recover the project.

Open-work register

Give unfinished items an owner, next action, and review date.

Handoff messages

Adapt delivery, incomplete-access, and requested-change messages to your project.

The files are editable Markdown and a nine-page printable PDF. The PDF is not an interactive form. Use a plain-text or Markdown editor; no particular app or online account is required.

Try it on one deliverable

  1. Download the free inventory worksheet and open it in a text editor.
  2. Describe one deliverable, its version, and who will maintain it.
  3. Confirm the intended recipient can open the link, then record the evidence of receipt.

Keep passwords, tokens, and other credentials out of the worksheet. Use your established secure access-transfer process separately. Opening a link yourself does not verify the recipient's access.

Usage and scope

The free preview is yours to adapt for your own work and include as a completed worksheet in a client handoff. The planned full kit permits the same use. Do not resell or redistribute the blank full toolkit as a separate template product.

This is an organizational aid. It does not transfer account access or legal ownership, certify security, replace a client agreement, or guarantee project acceptance. No custom implementation, consulting, software, or ongoing support is included.

The original material was prepared with AI assistance and reviewed for consistency. The worked example is fictional.
