What we check

Page responses, redirect destinations, titles, descriptions, canonical URLs, robots metadata and up to ten same-origin scripts, stylesheets or images per run.

External assets and assets with query strings are omitted. Rendered layouts, form submissions, logins and checkout are untested. A passing report describes only the checks performed.

Email alerts are not included in this pilot. Automatic results and issue indicators appear in your workspace. Checks are available once every 15 minutes per site.
