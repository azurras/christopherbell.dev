        Guide How to play, costs and odds

        The camp picture shows the shared world as it is now. Click the trees to gather wood, the rocks for stone, the dark forest edge to hunt, the fire to stand guard or rest, a tent to sleep or wake, a boat to sail away, and an animal to attack it. Glowing places are the moves you can make now; every move is also in the menus below the picture.

        Time is shared by the whole world: each 24-minute day has 14 minutes of daylight, 4 of dusk and 6 of night. At every nightfall raiders attack the camp. Survivors who stand guard at dusk add their attack damage to the defense; if the raiders are stronger they burn shelters and boats. Raids build through a seven-night week and then begin again. Nobody is hurt while away, but the shared camp is.

        In the 3D world, hogs roam peacefully. Wolves attack within seven metres and bears within ten, so give hostile animals a wide berth. Move within five metres of an available animal to Hunt. The camp illustration uses its own hunt and ambush rolls. Fleeing costs one bite.

        Walk to the Fishing bank and choose Fish. Each cast spends one fullness and can add one food if your pack has space.

        Gather stone (40% chance) to craft weapons. A club costs 2 wood (+1 damage, 8 uses), a stone axe 2 wood and 2 stone (+2 damage, 12 uses, wood gathering rises to 70%), a spear 3 wood and 3 stone (+3 damage, 12 uses, and its reach spares you the first bite of each fight). Each attack wears your weapon down until it breaks; crafting replaces the one you hold.

        Every effort outside camp, every combat move and every guard shift costs 1 fullness, and a night's sleep costs 2. Food restores 5 fullness and 2 health. At 0 fullness every effort costs 1 health and sleep does not heal.

        Each shelter has two beds. Sleep at dusk or night to recover 1 health for every 2 minutes asleep, or fully if you sleep until dawn. If raiders burn your shelter while you sleep, you wake with 2 damage.

        Share supplies chooses another survivor by name and camp ID. Give 1–10 wood, food or stone while both of you are at camp. Gifts move items directly into their inventory, which must have room; both survivors and the camp journal receive an update.

        Gather wood to improve strength and stamina. Your inventory holds 10 items. Five wood builds a shelter everyone can rest in; ten wood builds a boat any survivor can use to escape.

        Your attack deals 1 + strength + weapon damage, and the animal bites back on every attack, even the final blow. Defend blocks one bite. Defeating a hog or wolf gives 1 food and a bear up to 3, as your inventory allows. A shared shelter restores 2 health per rest.

        Sail away to survive, or start again after death. New survivor replaces only your character. Saved account characters can start again after death or escape; joining again resumes a living character. Shelters, boats, and other players remain in the shared world. Names and recent actions are visible to other players; use a nickname.

        Your logged-in account has one saved survivor. Moves, supplies, combat and the shared camp are saved after each successful command and survive server restarts. Log in again to resume. Guests use a temporary browser session and expire after two idle hours. Saved survivors keep their progress while inactive and return to the active camp when resumed. Guest progress stays separate from your account character. This version has no chat or matchmaking.

        Focus the island to walk with WASD or arrows. Drag or use Q / R to turn. Enter opens Commands, minus opens Main Menu, plus opens Chat log, and Escape closes a window. On a controller, use the sticks to walk and turn, the D-pad for menus, A to confirm, B to cancel, X for Main Menu, and Y for Chat log.
