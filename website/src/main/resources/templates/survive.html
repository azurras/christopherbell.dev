      Field guide · How to play

      Gather wood to improve strength and stamina. Your inventory holds 10 items. Five wood builds a shelter everyone can rest in; ten wood builds a boat any survivor can use to escape.

      Hunt to find hogs with 5 health. Your attack deals 1 + strength damage, and each attack costs 1 health, even the final blow. Defend blocks damage; flee returns you to camp at a cost of 1 health. Defeating a hog gives 1 food if you have room. Eating consumes it and restores up to 5 health. A shared shelter restores 2 health per rest.

      Sail away to survive, or start again after death. New survivor replaces only your character. Shelters, boats, and other players remain in the shared world. Names and recent actions are visible to other players; use a nickname.

      This world lives on one Java server. A server restart resets it; survivors expire after two hours without a game request. Closing the browser can end your survivor session. This version has no permanent saves, chat, or matchmaking.
